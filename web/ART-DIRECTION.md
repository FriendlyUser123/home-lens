# Art direction: Home Lens web

**Subject material:** a phone held over a desk. The viewfinder (corner brackets, a lens frame) and the paper it looks at
(a landlord's letter, a highlighter, an exhibit tag stuck to a photo). Blacksburg's Hokie stone grey is the desk.

**World:** one dark surface only: the camera. Everything after the shutter is paper on a stone-grey desk.
The lens frame (four yellow corner brackets) is the one repeating mark: it snaps onto the page, frames the deadline date,
and marks each matched photo. Highlights are real marker yellow, drawn left to right, and sit exactly on the printed words.

**Type:** Barlow Condensed (viewfinder numerals, the deadline) + Public Sans (form-grade, very legible). Both self-hosted.
No Inter, no serif display, no mono costume, no tracked-caps labels.

**Colour:** desk #D6D3CC, paper #FBFAF6, ink #17202B, marker #FFE45C (on paper only), lens yellow #FFD21F (on the dark
camera only), evidence amber #B45309 with AA text, check-this red #9E2A1B. Light page, because the use scene is daylight.

**Motion:** brackets glide to the page edge; highlights wipe in one by one; the page flattens with a 500 ms crossfade.
With prefers-reduced-motion everything appears at once.

**Avoiding (DESIGN-BRIEF.md):** dark warm-black page + cream serif headline; purple gradients, glow blobs, glass cards;
3-up icon card grid; eyebrow labels; corner concept tags; centred calm hero on every screen; side-stripe callouts; emoji icons;
paragraphs. One job per screen, headline 8 words or fewer, details behind "Why?".
