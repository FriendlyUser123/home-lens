# Home Lens

One camera for your home and your papers. Built at GDG Build with AI × Hacktoberfest 2026 (Virginia Tech, theme: AI for Housing).

- **Move-in:** photograph each room. Gemma 4 logs visible existing damage with timestamps, and you confirm every tag.
- **Problem:** photograph a leak, mold or broken heat. It drafts a written repair notice and starts Virginia's 14-day clock (Va. Code § 55.1-1244.1).
- **Move-out:** scan the deduction letter. Each charge is matched to your move-in photos, and you get the 45-day deadline (§ 55.1-1226) and a request letter with the proof attached.

**Model:** Gemma 4 (`gemma-4-26b-a4b-it`) via the Gemini API. Open-weight local mode: `gemma4:e4b` via Ollama.
Gemma terms: https://ai.google.dev/gemma/terms

Information, not legal advice.
