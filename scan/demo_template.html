What this does and does not do

  • It shows you the move-in view of a spot. It does not say whether something is damage, who caused it, or what it costs.
  • A photo is placed only when the walkthrough saw about the same view. In our tests roughly half of held-out photos were placed; the rest say "compare by eye" rather than guess.
  • The model may miss small damage. A short list is not a complete list.
  • The "same spot" check is the 3D position of the boxed spot; a spot more than the limit away from every tag is reported as having no tag.
